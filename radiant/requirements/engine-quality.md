# Engine and quality acceptance audit

Baseline: tracked Radiant source and recorded checks as of 18 November 2026. Sources are the report's [engine](../../report/src/pages/engine.html) and [quality](../../report/src/pages/quality.html) pages. Paths below are relative to `radiant/`. Priorities come from the blueprint. **Verified** means the recorded evidence covers the named check on its tested configuration. **Partial** means implementation or narrower evidence exists. **Open** means the requested capability or acceptance artefact was not found. **Later** retains the blueprint's scheduling. Audit only. Existing measurements are quoted with their original scope.

The matrix contains 40 engine and 64 quality requirements. Each row lists the evidence for its acceptance criteria and the environment in which it was collected.
