# Photosynthesis {#photosynthesis emphasis=2}

## Light reactions {#light side=right}
Happen in the thylakoid membranes.

- Photosystem II
- Electron transport chain
- ATP synthase {bold}

## Calvin cycle {#calvin side=left folded}
Happens in the stroma; fixes carbon dioxide.

- Carbon fixation {#fixation}
- Reduction
- Regeneration of RuBP

## Inputs and outputs {#io side=right}
- Water in, oxygen out
- Carbon dioxide in, sugar out
